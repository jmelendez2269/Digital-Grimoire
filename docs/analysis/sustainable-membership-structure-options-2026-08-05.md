# Sustainable Prismarium membership structure options

**Date:** August 5, 2026  
**Status:** Superseded for tier design by `docs/analysis/prism-credit-four-tier-model-2026-08-05.md`. Retained as a record of alternatives; no pricing or homepage changes authorized.

## Decisions that should hold across every option

1. **Courses are evergreen and optional.** They are structured ways to enjoy Prismarium, not prerequisites for using it correctly.
2. **Scholar includes every released course.** A researcher may ignore them, use one occasionally, or work through the entire archive.
3. **The product cannot depend on a constant creator feed.** Its recurring value must come from the Library, Knowledge Graph, Journal, saved research, Seven Lenses, Deep Search, and other maintained tools.
4. **Public content remains substantial, not exhaustive.** Prismarium can continue publishing complete public videos while reserving supplementary studio notes, previews, afterthoughts, or member conversations.
5. **Creator extras are bonuses unless a sustainable cadence is explicitly promised.** Do not make the price depend on weekly videos or posts.
6. **Adept remains reserved.** It should not be sold until it has a distinct applied-practice outcome.

## YouTube, sustainability, and pressure

The membership can retain its value without constant YouTube output. YouTube primarily affects **discovery and new-member acquisition**; it does not need to be the product customers are paying to receive every week.

The durable system is:

> publish a course or investigation once → preserve it as an evergreen public discovery asset → connect it to a maintained Prismarium course, source trail, workbook, and tools → let future learners continue using it

If YouTube pauses, the existing Library, course archive, Graph, Journal, and research tools still work. What may slow is the arrival of new people. Evergreen search pages, course landing pages, referrals, collaborations, and the existing video archive can continue acquiring users without a constant upload schedule.

### Recommended creator-content policy

Promise:

> Prismarium will continue to offer substantial free public videos and resources.

Preserve the option to make these member-only:

- Studio notes and behind-the-work updates.
- Early previews of courses, tools, and public videos.
- Extended or informal afterthoughts that are not needed to understand the public piece.
- Occasional member conversations, recordings, or research logs.
- Draft reading lists and works in progress.

Do not promise “one exclusive video every week” or a similar cadence unless producing it is already easy and enjoyable. Early access is lower pressure because it changes timing rather than requiring an additional piece of content.

## Option 1 — Guided Student, independent Scholar

This is the clearest long-term three-card structure.

| Plan | Price direction | Primary job | Main entitlement |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Reader | Free | Explore | Library, Graph, 50 Journal pages, free orientation, one AI demonstration |
| Student | $15 Founding; $19 target | Complete guided work | Every released course, course-linked tools, unlimited Journal, one independent inquiry/month |
| Scholar | $29 Founding; $39 only when ready | Research independently | Everything in Student plus generous self-directed tools and a full research workspace |

Scholar must receive concrete research freedom, not merely the word “Scholar”:

- Every released course, entirely optional.
- Early access to new courses and stable tools as a secondary perk.
- Full self-directed Seven Lenses and Deep Search access under fair use.
- Unlimited Library, Graph, ordinary search, Journal, annotations, and collections.
- Full research history, saved lens settings, continuity, and export.
- The Working under a measured allowance after it is repaired.
- Optional studio notes and previews as a bonus, without a promised publishing cadence.

**Strengths:** accessible course tier; strong tool-user tier; courses remain optional; no dependence on frequent YouTube.  
**Risk:** Scholar is not ready for $39 if its only reliable difference is a larger query counter. Keep it at $29 until the research workspace, metering, and at least one additional signature benefit are dependable.

## Option 2 — One paid All-Access membership for now

This is the most honest and simplest early-stage structure.

| Plan | Suggested price | Includes |
|---|---:|---|
| Reader | Free | Meaningful free foundation |
| Scholar / All Access | $29/month or $290/year initially | All courses, course tools, independent research fair use, unlimited Journal, Library, and Graph |

Student and Adept would be hidden temporarily. A lower Community Access price could preserve accessibility for people who cannot afford $29.

**Strengths:** no artificial feature gates; easiest homepage; easiest billing; the whole product is easy to explain; no obligation to generate constant creator content.  
**Risk:** removes the accessible $15 course-only step and gives up some price segmentation. Some people who mainly want courses may consider $29 too high.

Use this if the Student/Scholar boundary still feels invented when launch approaches. Reintroduce Student later only after real behavior proves that a course-focused segment needs a separate price.

## Option 3 — Evergreen course access plus ongoing research membership

This separates a finite learning journey from an ongoing tool relationship.

| Offer | Price direction | Includes |
|---|---:|---|
| Reader | Free | Public/free foundation |
| Student Course Library | $19/month or $190/year | All released courses, workbooks, course-linked tools, unlimited Journal |
| Scholar Research Membership | $39/month or $390/year | Everything in Student plus full independent research under fair use, research continuity, and early access |
| Optional single-course pass later | Approximately $79–$149 | One course and its course-scoped work; no ongoing independent research allowance |

The optional course pass helps people who want to complete one evergreen course without maintaining a subscription. It should be added only after annual billing and course-aware access are reliable.

**Strengths:** closely matches how people use evergreen courses; recurring Scholar revenue is tied to ongoing tool value; reduces pressure to publish constantly.  
**Risk:** more products and checkout paths; course-pass pricing and access expiration add operational complexity; some users may delay membership and buy only individual courses.

## Optional overlay — a low-pressure creator layer

Any of the three structures can include a small “Inside the Studio” area. It should not be a separate tier initially and should not carry a production quota.

Appropriate promise:

> Members may also receive occasional studio notes, early previews, and conversations from behind the work.

This gives room for Patreon-like material without promising that the membership is a weekly content feed. If an audience later demonstrates strong demand for creator access specifically, test an optional supporter contribution or Studio Circle separately from the product tiers.

## Comparison

| Criterion | Option 1: Student + Scholar | Option 2: One paid plan | Option 3: Course + research offers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage simplicity | Strong | **Strongest** | Moderate |
| Accessibility | **Strongest** | Weaker without discounts | Strong |
| Honest with current product | Moderate; Scholar needs work | **Strongest** | Moderate |
| Revenue segmentation | Strong | Weakest | **Strongest** |
| Depends on constant content | No | No | No |
| Engineering complexity | Moderate | **Lowest** | Highest |
| Fits evergreen courses | Strong | Strong | **Strongest** |
| Fits independent tool users | Strong | Strong | Strongest once Scholar is mature |

## Recommendation

Use a two-stage decision:

### Near term

Keep **Option 1 at the existing Founding prices: Free / $15 / $29**, remove Adept, and describe the distinction plainly:

- Student: the courses and every tool session needed to enjoy them.
- Scholar: everything in Student plus self-directed use of the research tools.

Do not raise Scholar to $39 until “self-directed research” is a materially better experience than simply receiving 25 instead of 5 queries.

### Escape hatch

If the Scholar experience still feels vague when the new pricing page is ready, use **Option 2** instead of inventing benefits. One honest $29 All-Access membership is stronger than three confusing paid promises.

### Later

Consider Option 3's single-course passes only after the course archive is mature and there is evidence that subscription-averse learners want them.

This sequence does not require constant YouTube. The creator channel can be active when inspiration and capacity allow; the product remains responsible for delivering the recurring value.
