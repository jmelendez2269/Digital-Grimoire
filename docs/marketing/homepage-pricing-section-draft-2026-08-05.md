## Choose your way into Prismarium

Read freely. Follow a guided path. Or use Prismarium as your independent research workspace.

Free public videos and resources will remain part of Prismarium. Membership is where you can follow the sources, save what you discover, and go further when you are ready.

**Billing toggle:** Monthly / Yearly — two months free

### The Reader

**Free forever**

For exploring at your own pace.

- Read the full Library.
- Explore the Knowledge Graph.
- Take the free orientation path.
- Save highlights, bookmarks, and collections.
- Keep up to 50 active Journal pages.
- Try one Seven Lenses or Deep Search inquiry.

**Button:** Start free

No credit card required.

### The Student

**$19/month**  
**$190/year — two months free**

For learners who want a path to follow.

- Access every released study path.
- Choose reading depths that fit your time.
- Use reading digests, exercises, and course workbooks.
- Use the tools your coursework calls for—those sessions are included.
- Keep an unlimited Journal.
- Explore one question of your own each month with Seven Lenses or Deep Search.

**Button:** Begin a study path

### The Scholar

**$39/month**  
**$390/year — two months free**

For independent researchers. No course required.

- Use Seven Lenses and Deep Search for self-directed research.
- Follow sources through the Library and Knowledge Graph.
- Save research history, lens settings, and connected notes.
- Keep an unlimited Journal and research workspace.
- Access every released study path whenever you want one.

**Button:** Research independently

**Recommended plan label:** Most complete Prismarium experience

### Short reassurance beneath the cards

The Library, Knowledge Graph, public writing, and a growing body of public videos remain free to explore. Paid memberships fund deeper tools, guided paths, and the continued growth of Prismarium.

### Small-print tool explanation

When a course sends a Student or Scholar to Seven Lenses or Deep Search, that course session is included and does not use an independent inquiry. Scholar includes generous self-directed use under a fair-use policy. Technical failures never count as a use.

## Homepage FAQ copy

### Will there still be free public content?

Yes. Prismarium will continue to offer substantial free public videos and resources. Some supplementary studio notes, previews, or member conversations may live inside Prismarium, while membership primarily adds a place to follow sources, complete guided work, use deeper research tools, and keep what you discover.

### Do I need to take a course?

No. Reader members can explore freely, and Scholar is designed to work as a standalone research membership. Courses are available whenever you want more structure.

### What does “course tools included” mean?

When an enrolled course asks you to use Seven Lenses or Deep Search, that session is included with the course and does not use your independent monthly inquiry.

### What counts as an independent inquiry for Student?

It is one Seven Lenses or Deep Search investigation that you begin outside an assigned course activity. Course-linked sessions are separate.

### Is Scholar unlimited?

Library reading, Knowledge Graph exploration, ordinary search, Journal pages, annotations, bookmarks, and collections are unlimited. Generative research tools include generous individual use under a fair-use policy so Prismarium can remain sustainable and accessible.
