# The Working — Model Bake-off (complete: structure · voice · holism · way-in · permission)

**Intention:** `success` (stand-in for "a new job aligned with my highest timeline")
**Same graph-grounded palette + same prompt for all four voices.**

The prompt now carries everything we learned:
1. **Structure** — Title / Timing / Gather / **Begin** / **Name your intent** / The rite / Close.
2. **Voice** — lead with meaning, speak to "you", frame to real life.
3. **Holism** — one through-line · write to the feeling, not just the goal · curate · close the loop.
4. **A way in** — an opening movement that crosses from ordinary time into the work (was missing).
5. **Permission / accessibility** — timing is "best, not only"; materials can be substituted or held in mind; meets a real person doing this tonight.
6. **The petition** — a beat where the practitioner speaks their own real situation and fear.
7. **The record** — the close hands off to writing it down and watching what unfolds (the lab-notebook).

Models: Claude Sonnet (local) · Qwen3-max (~14s) · DeepSeek v3.2 (~15–47s, variable) · Kimi k2-0905 (~31s).

> **Cost (direct Anthropic, per ritual ≈ 3,000 in + 700 out):** Sonnet 4.6 ≈ **$0.02** (2¢) · Haiku 4.5 ≈ **$0.007** (0.7¢, ~⅓ the price, ~2× faster). OpenRouter models are fractions of a cent but you preferred Claude's voice.

### Sonnet 4.6 — *The Crown Already Forming*  (~35s · ~$0.02)
**Through-line:** success is something you *are*, not something granted.

**Timing.** **Sunday carries this most fully** — the day of the Sun, light that doesn't argue for itself. Near the **Summer Solstice** (Litha) is strongest, when light isn't merely present but *used*. But any Sunday is enough — light a candle on an ordinary Sunday evening and the channel is open.

**Gather.**
- **A gold or yellow candle** — gold holds the worth Marcus Aurelius trusted: value that doesn't depend on anyone's recognition. Success is something you *are*.
- **Allspice** — money, power, strength as the *results of real effort*; success isn't luck, it's accumulated will.
- **Amber** — the Heliades' tears for Phaeton; the sun's fallen light. Proof you have already tried, already stretched.
- **The Sigel rune** (Sowilo), drawn by hand — the sun at noon, fire at full force.
- **Amazonite** — for the fear it won't come; it doesn't say *you're fine*, it says *keep going*.
- **A pinch of salt or a stone from outside** — Earth, where effort dissolves into form.

*Use what you have. The correspondence is the meaning, not the object.*

**Begin.** Stand still. Three slow breaths — three ties action to form. On the third exhale: *"I am crossing from ordinary time."* Pass your hands through candle smoke or open a window. You are marking a threshold.

**Name your intent.** Speak plainly — not "I want success" but the actual situation: the specific project or role, and the fear underneath (the waiting, the doubt you've done enough, the worry the opening will close before you reach it). Say it aloud or write it by hand.

**The rite.**
1. **Light the gold candle:** *"This light names what I am working toward. It does not wait to be told it is bright."*
2. **Hold the amber** — grief transformed by time. Acknowledge what this effort has already cost. Set it in the light.
3. **Sprinkle the allspice:** *"What I have built with effort, I now call forward. Strength into form. Work into result."*
4. **Place the Sigel rune** beneath the candle; trace it: *"Sun at its height. I am not hiding."*
5. **Hold the amazonite.** Feel where the fear lives; don't push it away: *"I am steadied. I keep going."*
6. **Place the salt/earth stone:** *"Jupiter and Earth, this working is grounded here — not in fantasy, but in what I am building."*
7. Sit until something settles — a breath deeper than the last is enough.

**Close.** Thank Jupiter's patience, the sun's unflinching quality, the earth that holds every effort: *"What belongs to me is finding its way. I release the rite and return."* Snuff the candle (don't blow it out). Then write it down — date, words, what you felt. Success unfolds across the days after; watch for the opening that looks small.

### Haiku 4.5 — *The Working of First Light*  (~17s · ~$0.007)
**Through-line:** success whispers before it roars.

**Timing.** Success isn't one season — it's built across many. To strengthen what's already moving, work on a **Sunday during the waxing Moon** — Sunday's solar clarity, growing light mirroring effort beginning to show. **Thursday** works too (Jupiter's expansion). If neither falls kindly, **any Sunday will do** — the principle holds whether the Moon is dark or full.

**Gather.**
- **Sigel rune** (drawn, or held in mind) — the sun at noon, the force that breaks through doubt.
- **Amber** — the Heliades wept it into being; sorrow turned into something that lasts.
- **Allspice** — Mars and effort joined; the taste of real work bearing fruit.
- **Alexandrite** — your truth made visible; it shifts as you do, never false.
- **The Chariot card** (drawn, sketched, or remembered) — controlled will moving forward.
- **Gold thread or cord** (any gold material; even imagined) — the sun's lineage made tangible.

*You need none of these things. If you have only your breath and voice, you have everything.*

**Begin.** Sit where light touches you — a window, an open door. Close your eyes for three breaths. On the fourth, open them and feel the light's warmth on your skin. Say aloud: *"I step from waiting into doing."* That's the threshold. You're in.

**Name your intent.** Speak plainly — not "I want success," but: what is the thing you're building? What are you afraid will fail? What effort are you holding that hasn't yet shown its shape? Use your own words. Success without a shape is just noise.

**The rite.**
1. Hold the **Chariot**: *"I am not drifting. I have chosen a direction."* Feel the difference between hoping and *deciding*.
2. Draw the **Sigel** rune on your palm: *"Sun at noon. No shadow. I call my own force into view."*
3. Light a candle; take the **amber**: *"The Heliades wept into amber — sorrow became what lasts. My struggle becomes endurance."* You're not denying the cost; you're making it *matter*.
4. **Taste the allspice** — let it burn slightly: *"Mars in my blood. Work, not wish."* Success tastes like something; it's not abstract.
5. Hold the **alexandrite**: *"I change as I need to. Truth shifts in light, and I follow it."* Permission to adapt — where most people fail.
6. Wrap the **gold thread** around your wrist; say three times: *"The sun has a lineage. I am part of it. What I build will last."*
7. Close your eyes. Sit in silence as long as feels true. Don't ask for anything else. The working is complete.

**Close.** Open your eyes slowly — back in ordinary time, but not unchanged. Write down what you *named* (not the ritual — the thing you're building). Tuck it where you'll see it. The working ends when you move — one real step. Watch the next week: which doors open, which fear quiets. *Success whispers before it roars. You'll recognize it.*

## 1. Claude Sonnet — *The Chariot's Turning*
**Through-line:** your worth is not the market's verdict.

**Timing.** Strongest on a **Thursday** — Jupiter's day of expansion and the right-sized door — or a **Sunday** if you most need to be *seen*. But any week holds this; do it the evening you're ready, not the perfect date. The work waits for you, not the calendar.

**Gather.**
- **Gold** (coin, ring, cloth) — worth that *is*, untouched by judgment.
- **Amazonite** — for showing up while change feels slow.
- **Allspice** — success as the fruit of real, ongoing effort.
- **Sigel/Sowilo** rune + **The Chariot** card — the sun at noon, controlled forward motion.
- A green candle / **Green** cloth — the moving leaves of cosmic order.

*If a piece is out of reach, hold its meaning in mind — the focus is the vessel, not the inventory.*

**Begin.** Clear a small space. Three slow breaths. Light the candle (or picture its flame). Say: *"I step out of the ordinary hour. The work begins."*

**Name your intent.** Speak your own, plainly — the real role you want, and the real fear beneath it: the silence after applying, the worry it won't come. *"This is the work I'm reaching for. This is what I'm afraid of. This is the effort no one sees but me."* Let it be unpolished.

**The rite.**
1. Hold the **Gold**: *"My worth is not their yes or their no. It is."*
2. **Amazonite** to your left — let it hold the fear you just named.
3. Three pinches of **Allspice** in a small spiral: *"Effort to yield. I do the work that is mine."*
4. Lay **Sigel** on the gold to break what's stuck; the **Chariot** above it. Speak the *shape* of the work — not the title.
5. Call **Jupiter**: *"Send the door that's rightly mine — and let it feed more than me."*

**Close.** Thank the light and your own stubborn hope. Return to the ordinary hour — a breath, hands to the floor. Keep the gold where you dress for work; carry the Amazonite until you take one concrete step, within three days. Write tonight down, and in the days after note what arrives — even if it's only the nerve to send one more message. When the door opens, give the allspice back to the earth.

## 2. Qwen3-max — *Rite of Steady Radiance*  (~14s)
**Through-line:** steady radiance doesn't shout; it shows up.

**Timing**
Perform on a **Sunday** — the Sun's own day, when clarity and presence blaze brightest. If timing must bend to your life, **any Sunday carries this light**; near the **Summer Solstice** that solar fire burns even stronger.

**Gather**
- **Alexandrite** — to align inner truth with outer action, so success isn't just won, but *lived*.
- **Allspice** — for the tangible results of persistent effort, not luck alone.
- **Amber** — the tear-born memory of ambition that fell too fast, hardened into wisdom.
- **SIGEL (Sowilo)** — the sun at its peak: breakthrough, visibility, unshaken confidence.
- **Gold cloth or candle** — success as alignment with what *is*, not just what you want.
- **A bowl of earth or a potted plant** — to root success in the body and the work that endures.

*(If any item is out of reach, hold its essence in mind — your focus is the true vessel.)*

**Begin**
Stand barefoot if you can. Breathe in silence for three breaths. Light the gold candle (or imagine its flame): *"I cross from shadow into light — not to chase glory, but to meet my own strength."*

**Name your intent**
Speak plainly, aloud: *"This is what I'm working toward… This is what I'm afraid will never come… This is the effort no one sees — but I do."* Let your voice shake if it must. Truth first; polish later.

**The rite**
1. **Amber** in your left hand: *"I honor the falls that shaped me."*
2. Sprinkle **allspice** into the earth bowl: *"Let my labor bear fruit — not someday, but in ways I can hold."*
3. **Alexandrite** to your heart: *"I will not hide my desire behind false modesty."*
4. Trace **SIGEL** in the air — upward stroke, then rays: *"My light is mine to claim."*
5. Hands on the earth: *"Success, meet me here — in my hands, my days, my honest work."*

**Close**
Blow out the candle. Thank the Sun, the Earth, and your own stubborn hope. Write down one sentence of what you asked for, and tuck it where you'll see it in three days. Steady radiance doesn't shout. It shows up, day after day, and *keeps working*.

## 3. DeepSeek v3.2 — *The Sunlit Seed*  (~15–47s, variable)
**Through-line:** tend the seed; watch for growth, not just outcomes.

**Timing**
The strongest window is any **Sunday**, the day of the Sun. For an added surge, align with the **Summer Solstice (Litha)** — but do not wait for a rare date; any Sunday carries this current, a reliable weekly anchor.

**Gather**
- **Allspice** — the real, ongoing effort that turns will into tangible results.
- **Amber** — Phaeton's fall and the Heliades' tears: fiery ambition grounded; what endures is born of transformation.
- **A small dish of soil or a houseplant (e.g. Aloe)** — Earth, the shared source; the quiet endurance of the pentacles.
- **The rune Sigel (Sowilo)** — the sun at its peak, to break through doubt.
- **Something gold or yellow** — the sun's divine light, unchanging truth, not mere praise.
- **A candle (any will do)** — to call the South's fire and raw energy for new beginnings.

*If you lack any item, hold its meaning in mind or name it aloud. The intention, not the inventory, fuels the rite.*

**Begin**
Light your candle — a small sun in your hands. Face **South**. Three deep breaths, drawing in Sunday's clarity, exhaling the fog of doubt. This is your threshold.

**Name your intent**
Speak aloud. Name the specific success — the real job, the tangible project. But also name the quiet fear: that your effort won't be seen, that your seed might not sprout. Speak your petition into the space between the flame and the earth.

**The rite**
1. Allspice in your palm: *"This is for the work, not the wish."* Place it before the candle.
2. Touch the soil/plant: *"I am rooted in what lasts."*
3. Draw the **Sigel** rune in the soil or air: *"I call on the sun's peak strength to illuminate my path and break what holds me back."*
4. Place the **amber** between soil and candle: *"Let my actions be tempered by wisdom, and let what grows endure."*
5. Hold the **gold**, circle it over your space: *"As this gold holds its worth beyond praise, so let my success be true to itself."*
6. Gaze at the flame; see your intent burning clearly. Let it burn in silence a few moments.

**Close**
Thank the fire of the South, the endurance of Earth, the clarity of solar light. Blow out the candle. Place the amber or allspice by your plant as an anchor. Turn from the South; breathe ordinary air. Write down what you felt and said — and in the days that follow, watch not just for outcomes, but for the quiet, steady growth of the seed you've planted.

## 4. Kimi k2-0905 — *The Quiet Harvest of What Must Be Done*  (~31s)
**Through-line:** meet the light halfway; the ritual walks beside you.

**Timing**
The golden hour of a **Sunday** is your strongest gate — sunlight at its most brazen. Near the **Summer Solstice**, Litha's noonfire carries your petition further. But any Sunday at hand will do; *the Sun does not withhold its rays for the sake of ceremony.*

**Gather**
- **Alexandrite** — your success is not a switch but a speaking of truths you've hidden even from yourself.
- **Allspice** — the scent of sweat and coin already earned; effort is the root, not the result.
- **Amber** — tears that turned to sun-stone: what we cry today becomes tomorrow's strength.
- **Sigel/Sowilo** — drawn on a scrap or traced in coffee grounds; the noon-rune that says "I choose to shine."
- **A single gold coin or disc** — the Pentacle of your work-in-progress, held in the hand rather than placed on an altar.

*Any of these may be improvised: a gold bead for the coin, the memory of allspice if the jar is empty, the rune traced on your skin. Hold the essence if the form is missing.*

**Begin**
Stand where sunlight can touch you — kitchen window, front stoop, rooftop. One slow breath in through the nose, out the mouth; slip off the day's noise like a coat. Whisper: *"I step into the clear between."*

**Name your intent**
Say aloud what success looks like for *you* this season — not the imagined title, but the actual sentence you want to speak at the next review, the amount in the account that ends worry for rent, the finished draft that can finally rest. Let the words be plain, even awkward.

**The rite**
1. Hold the **alexandrite**; confess what you keep silent: *"I fear I will not be seen."* Let the stone absorb the quiver.
2. Touch the **amber** to your collarbone where tears would fall; imagine them hardening into a warm, glowing bead ready to be spent.
3. Scatter **allspice** onto the coin in three taps, counting 1-2-3 — the real steps you already know you must take this week.
4. Draw **Sigel** over the coin: *"As the sun climbs, so do I."*
5. Place the coin in a patch of light — windowsill, phone case — where it's sun-touched daily until next Sunday. Leave the alexandrite beside it to keep the promise of honest speech.
6. At first dusk, pocket the coin as the Pentacle of work-in-progress; let amber warm against your skin each morning.

**Close**
Exhale, steady and audible. Thank the Sun for its ordinary, unstinting generosity: *"This week I will meet the light half-way."* Return to the day; the ritual now walks beside you. Tonight or tomorrow, jot down what first small success arrives — even if it is only the courage to speak the next true sentence.

## Verdict / decision

- **Everything the rituals were missing is now in the prompt** — a way in, permission (timing + materials), the practitioner's own petition, a grounding close, and the hand-off to the record. All promptable; all model-agnostic.
- **Kimi k2-0905** — best, most human voice ("the amount in the account that ends worry for rent"); most accessible. ~31s.
- **Qwen3-max** — excellent and fast (~14s); its three-line petition template is a reusable gem. Best speed/quality balance.
- **DeepSeek v3.2** — warmest, most thorough; fully complete now. But speed was variable this run (up to ~47s).
- **Sonnet** — strong; separate Claude API key + cost (not via OpenRouter).

**The prompt (structure · voice · holism · way-in · permission · petition · record) is the asset** — it becomes the canonical synthesis prompt in the assembly lib regardless of model.
